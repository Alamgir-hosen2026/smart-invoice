Privacy Policy

Smart Invoice Studio All in 1  |  Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Invoice Studio All in 1 (the "App"), published by Alamgir App Studio ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. We wrote it in plain language. In short: your business data stays on your phone, and we do not run a server that collects it.

Summary. We do not collect, sell or share your invoices, customers, payments, expenses, photos or signatures. The App has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play. Anything that leaves your phone does so only when you choose to share or back it up.

The App is developed and published by Alamgir App Studio, an independent developer based in Bangladesh. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at [your contact email].

3. No server, no tracking, no ads

  • The App does not send your business data to any server operated by us. We do not have a server that receives it.
  • The App contains no advertising and no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting or user-tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not use your information to build profiles, and we do not sell or rent it.
  • Automation, insights, forecasts, scores and reminders are calculated by rules running on your phone. No artificial-intelligence service or external API receives your data.

5. Receipt scanning (on-device text recognition)

The Receipt Scanner uses Google's ML Kit text recognition. The recognition model is bundled with the App and runs entirely on your device. Your receipt images and the recognized text are not uploaded to us or to Google by this feature.

6. Subscriptions and payments

Access to the App is provided through a subscription sold via Google Play Billing. Payments are processed by Google. We never see or store your card or payment details. Google tells the App whether your subscription is active so we can unlock or lock features. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions).

7. Sharing, exporting and backups

The App only sends information out of your device when you ask it to. Examples:

  • Sharing a document. When you share or send a PDF, reminder message or template, Android opens the app you pick (email, messaging, cloud drive, etc.). That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Backups. A backup is one ZIP file containing the data you select. It is stored on your phone (and can be saved or shared by you). Automatic backups stay on your phone. Backups are not encrypted by the App, so keep them somewhere safe and share them only with people you trust.
  • Password-protected PDFs. You can add a password to a PDF before sharing. This uses standard PDF encryption; choose a strong password and share it separately.
  • Client approvals. Signatures captured on screen are stored in the document on your device and appear in the PDF you share.

8. Your customers' information

When you enter information about your own customers, suppliers or employees, you are the one who decides what is collected and how it is used. You are responsible for having a lawful reason to store and use it, and for following the privacy and tax laws that apply to your business. We do not have access to that information.

9. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual records inside the App, delete a whole business from Settings → My businesses, or remove everything by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App in Android settings. Uninstalling the App removes its private data from your phone. Backup files that you saved or shared elsewhere are not removed automatically; please delete those yourself. Because we hold no copy of your data, we cannot recover it if you lose it without a backup.

10. Security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot normally read on a non-rooted device. We use standard Android protections and keep third-party code to a minimum. No method of storage is perfectly secure. We recommend that you use a screen lock, keep your phone updated and protect your backup files.

13. International users and your rights

You can view, edit, export (through backups and PDFs) and delete your data yourself inside the App. If you are in a region with data-protection laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR or California's CCPA/CPRA), you keep all rights those laws give you, and you can contact us at the address above with any question.
